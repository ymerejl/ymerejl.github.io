Why can't my AXIUM EX4000 terminal be started?
If your AXIUM EX4000 terminal cannot be started, it could be due to a low energy status or a defective state of an application running on the device. Ensure that the device is properly powered. You might also try resetting the device. If the problem persists, contact customer service for further assistance.

What should I do if there is a transaction processing failure with my AXIUM EX4000?
Transaction processing failure may occur due to various reasons such as damaged magnetic or smart cards, a faulty card reader, or improper card swiping. To troubleshoot, use more than one magnetic card for a transaction to confirm if the issue is with the card itself. Ensure that card swiping is done correctly. If a manual transaction works without a reader, the card reader might be at fault. Check the SIM card installation and the function of the local wireless communication network. Lastly, ensure the SAM card is properly inserted.

How can I reboot my AXIUM EX4000 terminal in case of an abnormal behavior?
If your AXIUM EX4000 terminal exhibits abnormal behavior, press and hold the power button for 8 seconds to force a reboot. This procedure will help resolve many common issues without further intervention.

How do I ensure the SIM and SAM cards are correctly installed in the AXIUM EX4000?
To ensure that the SIM and SAM cards are correctly installed, power off the terminal before opening the SIM card tray. The SIM/SAM connectors are located on the right side of the terminal. Insert the ejector into the tray hole to pop it out, then place the cards in their corresponding slots marked inside the tray. Note that SAM and SIM2 share a slot, so only one can be used at a time according to the terminal function.

What should I do if the AXIUM EX4000's card reader or transaction function fails to work?
If the card reader or transaction function of the AXIUM EX4000 terminal fails, verify if the card is damaged or improperly inserted. Use multiple cards to test the reader functionality. If manual transactions work, the issue might lie with the reader. Also, check that the device is connected to a functioning phone line or network, as this may affect transaction success.
