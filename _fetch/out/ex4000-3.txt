Product description
Redefining the Checkout Experience

The AXIUM EX4000 revolutionizes the checkout experience by offering a smart and reliable payment companion that empowers businesses to accept payments wherever they go. With its leading-edge Android apps and top level security features the EX4000 is the ultimate solution for businesses on the move.

Terminal Specifications
Robust and Featherweight: The AXIUM EX4000 combines Android and payment security in a robust yet featherweight design.
Power of Android 10: Experience the power of Android 10 along with optimized performance, speed and power-saving features.
Versatile Connectivity: Stay connected with 4G, 3G, GPRS, Bluetooth and WiFi capabilities allowing you to process payments on the spot.
Payment Versatility
Multiple Payment Methods: Accept payments in various ways including EMV Chip & PIN, contactless, magstripe or digital wallets.
QR-Code Based Payments: Enable QR-code based payments with the rear camera providing convenience and security for both merchants and customers.
Top-Level Security: Raising the bar on security with PCI PTS V6 certification ensuring that your transactions are safe and compliant with industry standards.
Supported by Ingenico’s Suite of Services
Fleet Management & Security Solutions: Benefit from Ingenico’s suite of services including fleet management and security solutions.
Professional Services: Access professional services to enhance your AXIUM EX4000 experience including hardware services and support for development, maintenance and updates.
Power, Performance and Reliability
Memory and Processor: Choose from multiple memory and processor options to suit your business needs.
Display and Keypad: Enjoy a large 5’’ touchscreen display and ergonomic interface for enhanced usability along with a physical keypad for easy navigation.
Connectivity and Data Capture: Stay connected with WAN, LAN, Bluetooth and WiFi connectivity options while capturing data effortlessly with rear camera and GPS capabilities.
Secure, Reliable, and Environmentally Friendly
PCI PTS V6 Certified: Rest assured that your transactions are secure and compliant with PCI PTS V6 certification.
Environmentally Friendly: With optimized battery life and sustainable development practices the AXIUM EX4000 is a sustainable solution for businesses on the go.
